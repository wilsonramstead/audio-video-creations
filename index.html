What we do

Everything your ride's electronics need

From a factory-stereo upgrade to a full custom sound system, cameras, screens and safety tech — it all gets dialed in right, in one shop.

Stereos & Head Units

Aftermarket touchscreen stereos with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto — from a clean single upgrade to a full dash overhaul on any vehicle, old or new.

Subs, Amps & Speakers

Custom subwoofers, amplifiers and speaker upgrades — slim, tucked-away installs that keep your cargo space and still deliver serious bass.

Dash Cams

Front and rear dash cam systems, cleanly wired and hidden, so every drive is recorded and you've got the footage when it matters.

Backup & Safety Cameras

Fully wired backup cameras and safety views integrated into your screen — park and reverse with a clear picture, not guesswork.

OEM Integration & Fixes

Adding to factory systems, keeping steering-wheel controls, and cleaning up hacked or half-done past installs so it all works the way it should.

Interlock Service

Professional, respectful ignition interlock service and support — handled the right way, with the patience customers keep pointing out.
